Instructions for subscribing to the Howling Hours calendar
Calendar Instructions
Google Calendar 1. Press Copy calendar link above.
2. Press Open Google Calendar.
3. Find Other calendars on the left.
4. Press + and choose From URL.
5. Paste the copied calendar link.
6. Press Add calendar.
Apple Calendar Press Apple / Calendar App above. Your device should offer to subscribe to the Howling Hours calendar. If it does not, copy the calendar URL and add it as a subscribed calendar manually.
Outlook Copy the calendar link above. In Outlook, look for Add calendar and then an option such as Subscribe from web or From Internet. Paste the calendar URL there.
Other calendar apps Copy the calendar subscription link and look for an option such as Subscribe by URL, Calendar URL, Internet calendar, or Web calendar.

Important: subscribe using the calendar URL instead of importing the downloaded .ics file. Importing creates a one-time copy. Subscribing allows future schedule changes to appear automatically.

Google, Apple, Outlook and other calendar services decide how often they refresh external calendars. Changes may therefore take some time to appear after I update the schedule. The live Howling Hours website will always show the newest availability first.
