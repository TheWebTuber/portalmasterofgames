CAMPAIGN INTENT

Long means long.

This project is specifically looking for people who want a campaign that can continue for the long term. It is not being advertised as a one-shot or a mini-campaign.

There is no promise that real life will never interrupt a campaign — schedules change — but the intention from the start is continuity.

TABLE SIZE

Interest first. Seats second.

Joining the Discord means “I am interested in talking,” not “I am guaranteed a seat.” The final group size and roster should be decided together once a DM is involved.

If several good people appear, that is a good problem to have. Nobody needs to be rushed into a commitment.

Still seeking the right DM.

DUNGEON MASTER WANTED

Want to build the road with us?

We are actively looking for a DM who genuinely wants to run a long-running recurring campaign, not simply cover a few sessions and disappear. You do not need a finished world or campaign document today — but you should be interested in putting real, sustainable effort into making the table worth returning to.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

A DM who wants continuity.

  • Can usually work with one of the listed UK-time windows and sessions of roughly three hours.
  • Wants a recurring campaign intended to continue long-term rather than a fixed short mini-series.
  • Communicates reliably about schedule changes, breaks, boundaries, and availability.
  • Is willing to prepare enough to keep sessions coherent, fun, and moving without turning DMing into a second job.
  • Enjoys collaborative worldbuilding, character stories, consequences, jokes, and letting players affect the direction of the game.
  • Is comfortable discussing a public YouTube livestream before play begins.
  • Is willing to run an unstreamed Session 0 / group conversation first.

WHAT THE DM GETS A SAY IN

This is not a pre-written cage.

  • Edition, setting, campaign premise, tone, house rules, and character-creation limits should be decided with the DM.
  • The DM should have a real voice in final player count and whether the table feels manageable.
  • Content boundaries and safety expectations are discussed before public play.
  • Breaks are allowed. “Long-running” does not mean “never miss a week for the rest of your life.”
  • The organizer can handle Discord organization and livestream/technical setup so the DM does not have to carry every job.
  • No one should expect the DM to personally fund books, VTT subscriptions, assets, or other campaign costs without prior agreement.

FAQ

Read this before summoning the questions.

Is this definitely a long-running campaign?

That is the intention. The group is being built for recurring long-term play, not a fixed three-session or short-season format. No campaign can promise that nobody's life will ever change, but the starting goal is continuity.

Which day will the campaign actually use?

Friday, Saturday, and Sunday are the current possible windows. Saturday or Sunday offers the most normal evening hours. The final recurring day will be chosen with the DM and actual players rather than imposed before the group exists.

Does joining Discord mean I am accepted?

No. It means you are interested enough to talk. Everyone should have a chance to meet first, discuss expectations, and see whether the group actually fits.

Do you already have a DM?

Not yet. We are actively looking for one. Interested players can still join the discussion while the DM search is happening, and a potential DM is welcome to join simply to talk before making any commitment.

Is the DM expected to be free or paid?

A volunteer / free DM is strongly preferred. This is intended to be a long-running hobby campaign, and a recurring per-session fee can become a significant cost over months or years. A paid DM is not the current plan. If the group ever considers that route, cost and expectations should be discussed openly with everyone first rather than appearing as a surprise later.

What kind of commitment are you asking from a DM?

Consistent communication, reasonable preparation, and genuine interest in building a campaign that can continue. We are not asking a DM to make D&D a second full-time job. Breaks, schedule changes, and real-life limits are normal; the important part is communicating them and wanting to return to the table.

Will I be livestreamed immediately?

No. Streaming should be discussed before anyone commits. The intended path is an unstreamed conversation / Session 0 first, where everyone can discuss boundaries, voice/video preferences, VODs, clips, and what public participation actually means.

Why use UK time?

It gives the invitation a clear public scheduling reference without requiring the organizer to publish a more specific personal location. Always interpret the schedule as current UK local time, including GMT/BST changes.

Can I forward this to someone else?

Yes — if you genuinely think they might fit. Send them the page and let them decide for themselves. Please do not pressure people or give out private contact details without permission.

Can multiple people from the same friend group show interest?

Absolutely. More genuine interest is useful. It simply does not guarantee that every interested person will end up in the final player roster.

What edition, setting, rules, and campaign premise are being used?

Those details should be finalized with the DM and group. This page recruits for the social and scheduling structure first, rather than pretending the entire campaign has already been decided without its DM.
