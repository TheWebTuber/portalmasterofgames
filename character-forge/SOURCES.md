# Character Forge rules sources

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 (“SRD 5.1”) by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The bundled catalog adapts the [English SRD 5.1](https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.1/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf) into selectable options and numeric inputs. New labels, formatting and summaries are adaptations. These options use the 2014 rules.

Race entries give fixed increases, walking speed and trait reminders. Subrace entries combine their base race and subrace. Half-elf flexible ability increases, language choices, spells and similar player selections belong to the player. Class defaults describe a single class, its saving throws, Hit Die and its SRD subclass. The weapon list gives base damage for a normal attack with a damaging weapon. Versatile weapons default to one-handed damage. Use an override for alternate damage, flat damage bonuses or unusual attacks. The net has special rules and deals no weapon damage.

The [2014 community rules directory](https://dnd5e.wikidot.com/) provides optional reference links for other published and homebrew choices. The app does not redistribute the directory’s descriptions or illustrations. A manual entry’s source and reference can identify which rules the table uses.
